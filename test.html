Module 01

Essay Types & Rhetorical Structure

Explore core structures essential for academic writing proficiency.

Cause & Effect

Examines the reasons behind an event or condition and analyzes the resulting consequences.

  • Key Signal Words: Consequently, Due to, Thereby
  • Focus: Causal Relationships

Compare & Contrast

Analyzes similarities and differences between two or more subjects to provide deeper understanding.

  • Key Signal Words: Whereas, Conversely, Likewise
  • Focus: Synthesis & Distinction

Summarization

Condenses lengthy texts into core thesis points while maintaining objective neutrality and clarity.

  • Key Signal Words: In essence, In summary, Ultimately
  • Focus: Brevity & Precision

Academic Term
Articulate

/ɑːrˈtɪk.jə.leɪt/

Flip card
Verb / Adjective

To express an idea clearly and fluently in speech or writing.

"She was able to articulate the complex arguments effectively."

Academic Term
Elucidate

/iːˈluː.sə.deɪt/

Flip card
Verb

To make something clear; to explain in detail.

"The presenter used diagrams to elucidate the underlying thesis."

Academic Term
Coherent

/kəʊˈhɪə.rənt/

Flip card
Adjective

Logical and consistent; forming a unified whole.

"A coherent essay requires smooth transitions between body paragraphs."
